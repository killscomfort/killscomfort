## Exact steps

1. **artofwhere.com → Sign up** (free) → in your account, click
   **Sign up for Wholesale and Drop Ship** → agree. Drop-ship pricing activates
   immediately.
2. **Create → Boyshorts** (artofwhere.com/create/boyshorts) — opens the
   Design Lab with a live 3D preview.
3. **Upload `back-panel-black.png`** and map it to the **back panel**.
   The logo is pre-positioned upper-center; nudge in the 3D preview until it
   sits across the seat the way your sourcing board shows. Fill the remaining
   panels (front, gusset, waistband) with solid black — either upload
   `bg-solid-black.png` to each or use the Lab's color fill with pure black
   `#000000`.
   - *Freeform alternative:* fill all panels black, then place
     `logo-chrome-transparent.png` on the back panel yourself and scale to
     taste (target: roughly 80% of panel width).
4. **Check the 3D preview from the back at an angle** — spiky logo tips should
   not wrap into side seams. If they clip, scale down ~10%.
5. **Save the product**, then **order**: quantities XS×1, S×1, M×2, L×1, XL×1,
   ship to your Miami address. Confirm the line total shows the **$14.70**
   tier before paying.
6. While you're in there: peek **Custom Labels & Packaging** — branded package
   stickers and artist cards are cheap and worth adding to the launch orders
   later (not needed for samples).

## When the box arrives (QC)

- **Black depth** — peachskin sublimation black should be deep; if it reads
  washed/charcoal, tell me and I'll rebuild the files with an adjusted black point.
- **Chrome fidelity** — the metallic grays are the print's hardest test.
- **Logo position & size** at S vs XL (placement drifts across sizes on AOP).
- **Fit vs their chart** (runs: XS 24-26" waist → XL 37-42").
- **Wash test one pair** — cold, low tumble — before anything goes public.

## After samples pass

Shopify Basic → install the free **Art of Where app** → publish the boyshort →
copy the product URL into `CHECKOUT_URL` in `booty-shorts.config.ts` → deploy.
Orders then flow automatically: customer pays on Shopify → AOW makes and ships
from Montreal. Landing page never changes.
